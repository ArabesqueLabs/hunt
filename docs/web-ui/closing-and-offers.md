# Closing, and an accepted offer

A process closes in a terminal stage, which says why; an accepted offer
wraps up the rest of the search, and hunt celebrates it with you.

## Closing is a stage

- A stage can be a terminal stage (Settings → Board). A process in a
  terminal stage is closed, and the sub stage it's in says why.
- A new hunt has three terminal stages, in Terminated Processes, whose sub
  stages are the reasons:
  - My Decision: I withdrew, I declined the offer … I started the job;
  - Their Decision: They rejected me, Role filled …;
  - Paused / Stopped: On hold, They went silent ….
- Add a reason as a sub stage, or another terminal stage. Make any stage
  terminal, and what's in it closes; not terminal again, and it opens.
- A closed process keeps the last stage it reached before ("Got to
  Interviewing"), which Stats and Cycles count it at.
- Drop a card on a reason to close it for that. Drag it back onto another
  column to reopen it, or Reopen it from its page: back to the stage it
  reached.

## Accepting an offer

- On an application at its offer (the Offer stage, or the last),
  "🎉 Accept the offer…" moves it to the stage's Accepted lane, with a
  note (a start date, say).
- Accepting isn't an ending: references, checks and the start may still be
  ahead, so it stays on the board until it's closed as "I started the
  job". A stage without an Accepted lane closes it as that straight away.
- Then the other open processes are wrapped up, each as chosen, or all at
  once:
  - closed (as withdrawn, noting the offer);
  - kept open, with a reminder for tomorrow to tell the recruiter (the
    default from Screening on);
  - or left as they are.

## The celebration

- From then on the home page celebrates the offer: the role, pay and note;
  below, how the search went (days from the first application to a yes,
  applications, companies, interviews, people, offers); and the people on
  the process, to thank.
- "Back to the board" returns the board to the home page (in this
  browser); "Your offer" in the sidebar shows the celebration again.

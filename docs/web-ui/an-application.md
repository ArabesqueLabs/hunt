# An application's page

Every application has a page of its own (`/applications/ID`): a card on
the board and a row in Applications both open it.

## What it shows

It reads first, top to bottom:

- its stage (a stepper), and whether it's archived;
- the role: location, work mode and employment first, then pay (in GBP
  first, with the original currency and rate beneath; bonus and equity),
  then the posting link, source and when you applied;
- the timeline: interview steps (when, format, with whom, notes) and what
  happened, in date order. "With whom" picks people from People, or takes
  a new name, which becomes a person (at the application's company) when
  the page is saved; reading, their names link to them;
- your notes;
- About the job: its postings, each laid out like an email (where it's
  from, when it was posted and closes, or last read, then headings, lists
  and links in its own box). Their links are under Posting links, beside,
  each with Refresh, Edit (its address, in a dialog) and Delete;
  Refresh from a posting, in the menu at the top, asks which when there's
  more than one;
- people, with their email and phone to click (to write or call); emails,
  as threads (by Gmail's thread, or by subject), folded until opened, each
  message with its time; links, each by its name if it has one, and
  changed, moved or removed from its ⋯ menu; attached files, the latest
  three as thumbnails, each with Edit (another version sent, or a new one
  uploaded, in a dialog) and Detach, and **View all files** for the rest
  in Files;
- the company, folded until opened, and the change log;
- tags, then Import from link…, Archive… and Delete at the bottom.

Every section folds from its header, and stays as you left it. The
timeline is a line an entry, beside the interviews as cards; **Full
timeline**, beneath it, opens it whole. The role's details and posting
links take a narrow column, with About the job in a wide one beside.

## Adding and editing

- Reading, the page shows no forms: **Add**, at the top before Stage,
  adds a note, an interview or a reminder to the timeline, each in a
  dialog; "+ Add a person", "+ Add a link", "+ Add" (a posting link)
  and "+ Link file" open theirs. Linking a file takes one from Files, or
  uploads one there and then.
- Each section's **Edit** turns it into a form: change it, then **Save**
  or **Cancel**. Leaving with unsaved changes asks first.
- An interview's or a note's **Edit** (in its ⋯ menu) opens a form for it
  alone, in a dialog.
- People, emails and files are saved as soon as they're added or taken
  off, in either mode.

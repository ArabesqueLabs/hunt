# Companies and people

The companies you apply to and the people you meet have lists and pages of
their own, and can be read in from their sites and LinkedIn.

## Lists and pages

- **Applications, Companies, People:** searchable lists; every record
  opens for editing, with its own change log.
- **A company's page** lists its applications, each with its stage (that
  of its newest active process, or else its newest, archived, and why) and
  its tags; then the people there.

## Importing a company or person

"Import from link…" on a new company or person, on their page, or in a
process's Company panel.

- It reads the company's site or LinkedIn page for its name, website,
  LinkedIn page, industry, size, headquarters, year founded and
  description. A levels.fyi company page (`levels.fyi/companies/stripe`)
  works too.
- It shows what it found next to what's there. Empty fields are ticked;
  filled ones are kept unless you tick them.
- Nothing is saved until you choose.
- LinkedIn sometimes refuses pages without a sign-in. When it does, it
  says so; use the company's own site instead.

## People from LinkedIn

Profiles need a sign-in, so hunt reads them from your browser.

- With the [browser extension](../../extension/README.md), on someone's
  profile, choose **Add to hunt's people** in its popup (or **Add this
  person to hunt** in the right-click menu).
- hunt opens with their name, role, company, location, about and photo to
  choose from, and updates whoever already has that profile instead of
  adding them twice.
- Someone of the same name, added by hand or under another link, is asked
  about, merged into, or left alone, as Settings → Connected apps say.
- The extension only collects the page's text; hunt reads it, so a change
  to LinkedIn's pages needs a hunt update, not a new extension.

## Refresh from LinkedIn

A company or person with a LinkedIn link has it in place of "Import…" (and
as refresh in the listings), reading that link again. LinkedIn keeps most
profiles from anyone not signed in; then it offers to open the profile,
for the browser extension.

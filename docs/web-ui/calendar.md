# The calendar

Interviews and application deadlines of active processes (`/calendar`), by
month, week, day or as an agenda, searched by company, role or title.

![The calendar, by month: interviews, next actions and deadlines](../screenshots/calendar-light.png#gh-light-mode-only)
![The calendar, by month: interviews, next actions and deadlines](../screenshots/calendar-dark.png#gh-dark-mode-only)

## Weeks and days

- The week and the day run midnight to midnight, each event at its time.
- The hours outside your working hours are hatched, and other time zones'
  hours can run beside yours: both in Settings → Calendar, which the icon
  by the page's title opens.
- "Weekends" shows or hides Saturdays and Sundays.

![A week: each event at its time, the hours outside working hours hatched](../screenshots/calendar-week-light.png#gh-light-mode-only)
![A week: each event at its time, the hours outside working hours hatched](../screenshots/calendar-week-dark.png#gh-dark-mode-only)

## What it shows

- Interviews already held are quieter, and cancelled ones are left out.
- A deadline shows until you apply.
- A key under the calendar says what its colours mean.
- Everything opens in its own view, over the calendar, under its company
  and role, with "Open application" to go to it:
  - an interview: when, the format, with whom, where, its links, what to
    prepare and how it went;
  - a next action: when, and whether it's overdue;
  - a task: its kind, status, priority, how long it takes, its interview,
    link and description, and "Open in Tasks";
  - a deadline: how long until it, and the posting.
- On an application's page, "Open in calendar" beside an interview's
  format opens it here, on its day.

## In the address

The view and the weekend choice are in the address
(`/calendar?view=week&weekends=hide`), so Back undoes a change, and
they're remembered. So is what's open in its view
(`/calendar?date=2026-11-03&event=step-12`), so it can be bookmarked.

## Coming up

"Coming up", the bell at the top of every page, is what's due today and
tomorrow: reminders (the overdue ones too, in amber), interviews, and
applications closing. How many is on the bell's corner (amber when one's
overdue); each opens its application, and "Open the calendar" the agenda.

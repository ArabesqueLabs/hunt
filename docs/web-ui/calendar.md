# The calendar

Interviews and application deadlines of active processes (`/calendar`), by
month, week, day or as an agenda, searched by company, role or title.

## Weeks and days

- The week and the day run midnight to midnight, each event at its time.
- The hours outside your working hours are hatched, and other time zones'
  hours can run beside yours: both in Settings → Calendar, which the icon
  by the search opens.
- "Weekends" shows or hides Saturdays and Sundays.

## What it shows

- Interviews already held are quieter, and cancelled ones are left out.
- A deadline shows until you apply.
- A key under the calendar says what its colours mean.

## In the address

The view and the weekend choice are in the address
(`/calendar?view=week&weekends=hide`), so Back undoes a change, and
they're remembered.

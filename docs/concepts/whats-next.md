# Interviews, reminders and prep

What's coming up for each process: the interviews, what you have to do,
and what to get ready.

## Interview steps

A step is one interview, or anything scheduled with the company: a
recruiter screen, a take-home, an onsite. It has its title, when and how
long, where (a joining link or an address), who it's with, and what to
prepare. Its status follows it through: scheduled, done and waiting, done,
passed, failed or cancelled.

Steps are on the process's timeline, and on the Calendar.

## Reminders

A reminder is something to do for a process, by a date: chase the
recruiter, send availability, send references. It's done once you tick it.

The card shows its next one, overdue ones turn amber, and what's due today
and tomorrow is under **Coming up**, in the sidebar on every page.

## The calendar

The Calendar has the interviews and the dates applications close, by
month, week, day or as an agenda, with your working hours shaded. A
private link puts them, and your reminders, in your calendar app
(Settings → Calendar): anyone with the link can see it, so it can be
turned off, or replaced with a new one.

## Tasks

Tasks are what to read, practise and rehearse: for a process, for one of
its interview steps, or in general. A daily one (a coding exercise, say)
keeps a streak. The Tasks page can show one application's (search for it
under **For**), and the ones not done yet are on the calendar, on their
own date or their interview's day.

**Stories** are for behavioural questions: things that happened at work,
as situation, task, action and result, written once and told in any
interview that asks.

## Links

A process's links are the pages that go with it: its candidate portal,
prep material, an interview's invite.

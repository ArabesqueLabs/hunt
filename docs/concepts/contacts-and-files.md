# Companies, people and files

What your applications have in common: the companies you apply to, the
people you meet, and the documents you send.

## Companies

A company is an employer or a recruitment agency, with its website,
LinkedIn page, industry, size, headquarters and description. Each name is
one company, whatever its capitals, and an application names its company.
A company's website or LinkedIn page fills in its details, and its logo.

## People

A person is a recruiter, hiring manager, interviewer or referrer, at a
company or not. A process lists the people involved and what each is to
it; an interview step lists who it's with. A LinkedIn profile fills a
person in, from a link or the browser extension.

Companies and people are your contacts, shared by every cycle.

## Documents and versions

A document is one of your files, kept up over time: your CV, a cover
letter, a portfolio, a reference, or one of theirs (an offer, a contract).

- Each upload is a new **version**, numbered, tagged ("rust-focused") and
  noted. A version never changes, so you always know what you sent.
- A process records **which versions went with it**, and a version, which
  processes it went to.
- Files are kept in the database, so a backup has them too.

## Tags

Tags go on applications, companies, people and documents, and the board
and the lists filter by them: "dream job", "via Alice", "fintech".

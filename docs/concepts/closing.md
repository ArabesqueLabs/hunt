# Closing and reopening

A process ends one way or another: an offer you take, a rejection, a role
you turn down, a company that goes quiet. In hunt, closing is a move.

## Closing is a stage

A process is closed when it's in a terminal stage, and the sub stage it's
in says why. Drop a card on "They rejected me" and it's closed, for that
reason. A new hunt's reasons are:

- **My Decision:** I withdrew, I declined the offer, I accepted another
  offer, I started the job …
- **Their Decision:** They rejected me, Role filled …
- **Paused / Stopped:** On hold, They went silent …

They're sub stages like any other: add your own reasons, or another
terminal stage, in Settings → Board. Make an active stage terminal and
what's in it closes; make it active again and it reopens.

## Closed isn't gone

A closed process keeps everything: its steps, notes, people and files, and
the furthest stage it reached, which Stats and Cycles count it at.

**Reopen** it from its page, or drag it back onto an active column. Reopen
puts it back in the stage it had reached.

## An accepted offer

Accepting an offer moves the process to its stage's Accepted lane, with a
note (a start date, say). It's not closed yet: references, checks and the
start may be ahead, so it stays on the board until you close it as "I
started the job". Then hunt helps wrap up the rest of the search (close
the others, or keep them open with a reminder to tell the recruiter), and
celebrates with you.

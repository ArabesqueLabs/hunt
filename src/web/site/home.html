Everything a job search has, kept straight

From the first link you save to the offer you accept.

  • A board of every application

    Each stage of hiring is a column, in swimlanes you lay out: what's active, the offers, what's closed. Divide a stage into sub stages, fold what you don't need to see, and drag cards along; or switch to a table to filter, sort and change many at once, with Undo.

  • Import a job from its link

    Paste a posting from LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable or levels.fyi, and hunt fills in the role, company, pay and description. Several links for one job are compared side by side.

  • Companies and people

    Recruiters, interviewers and referrers, with the companies they work at, and each application they're part of. Email or call them in a click, or bring them in from LinkedIn.

  • Interviews and next steps

    Every interview, task and follow-up has a date. See them on a calendar, by month, week or day around your working hours, in your own calendar app through a feed, and on each card as what's next.

  • Notes, files and versions

    Keep CVs and cover letters, with their versions, and which one went where. Notes and every change sit on each application's timeline.

  • See how it's going

    Stats show where applications stand and where they stop, which sources work best, and how long things take: to a first interview, and to an answer.

  • An offer, accepted

    Accept an offer and hunt wraps up the rest: close the other applications, or plan a note to each recruiter. Then it celebrates with you.

  • In your language, and currency

    English or Arabic, right to left. Your time zone, your way of writing dates and numbers, and pay shown in your currency at today's rates.

  • A command line, too

    Everything the board does, from your terminal, through the same API, so scripts and the browser always agree. And a backup of everything, in one file.

RTL Support

hunt is written for Arabic as carefully as for English: the whole page turns round, numbers can be written in Arabic numerals, and dates follow your region.

Free, open source, and yours to run

hunt is free software, under the GNU AGPL v3. Run it on your own computer or server, with SQLite, PostgreSQL or MySQL, in a container or on Kubernetes. Run it for friends, or a whole team, with each person's search their own.

Deleting your data

Last updated {{updated}}

Delete it yourself

You can delete your hunt account, and everything in it, at any time:

  1. Sign in to hunt, and open Settings.
  2. Under Your account, choose Delete my account…
  3. Type your email address to confirm, and choose Delete for good.

Your account and everything you kept in hunt (applications, companies, people, notes, files, settings and their history) are deleted at once, and from our backups as they are replaced. It can’t be undone, so download a backup first (Settings → Backup) if you might want any of it.

Or ask us

If you can’t sign in any more, or would rather we did it, email {{email}} from the address you sign in with, saying you’d like your hunt account deleted. We’ll delete it within one month, usually much sooner, and tell you when it’s done. A request for an account that hasn’t been approved yet can be deleted the same way.

Signing in with Facebook, LinkedIn, GitHub or Google

When you sign in with one of these, hunt receives only your email address and, if it shares it, your name, through Cloudflare Access. Deleting your hunt account deletes those too: hunt keeps nothing else from them.

To stop the service you signed in with from letting hunt know who you are, remove hunt’s access in that service’s settings as well: on Facebook, under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
